Special Unit: Redial -- third-party components and credits
==========================================================

1. Special Unit
   ------------
   Special Unit is a free Stargate SG-1 total conversion for Unreal Tournament 2004,
   (c) the SU Team (2013, https://www.moddb.com/mods/special-unit). Its readme
   forbids re-releasing it without the team's permission: nothing of the mod is in
   this project, neither its packages, maps and textures nor script exported from
   them. The installer makes you download the official SpecialUnit.zip from ModDB
   and checks its SHA-256. SUBotFix and SUTweaks are separate packages that load the
   mod's classes at run time and never change its files.

   The screenshots in docs/images come from the mod's ModDB page. Artwork (c) the
   SU Team.

2. Unreal Tournament 2004 and the OldUnreal patch
   ----------------------------------------------
   SUBotFix and SUTweaks are built with the UT2004 compiler (ucc make) against the
   game's own packages, which are not included. The installer offers the OldUnreal
   community patch (https://github.com/OldUnreal/UT2004Patches), downloaded from its
   GitHub releases.

3. Master servers
   --------------
   The master server list written by the installer is the one of the OldUnreal
   3374 patch (333networks, gonespy, errorist, frag-net, noccer, openspy).
